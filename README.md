## Case 3 : Wanderlust Travel

**Building a Metrics Dashboard for Wanderlust Travel**

Prerana Joshi is a talented Product Manager working for Wanderlust Travel, a leading online travel company. As the Product Manager on the Search team, Prerana faces the exciting challenge of building a metrics dashboard to enhance the search experience for users and drive business growth.

Wanderlust Travel is a well-established player in the online travel industry, catering to a global customer base. Offering a comprehensive range of travel services, including flights, hotels, and vacation packages, the company has gained significant market share. However, with mounting competition and evolving customer expectations, the Search team needs to constantly innovate and improve the search experience.

Prerana joins the Search team at Wanderlust Travel, which is responsible for optimising the search functionality on the company's website and mobile app. Currently, the team faces challenges due to a lack of a centralised metrics dashboard. Data analysis is performed manually, and insights are scattered across various systems and tools. This fragmented approach slows down decision-making and hinders the team's ability to identify and address key areas of improvement.

To overcome existing challenges and enhance the team's efficiency, Prerana recognizes the critical need for a comprehensive metrics dashboard. The following requirements must be considered when designing the dashboard:

**1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):** Prerana understands that the dashboard should present essential KPIs – these metrics will provide a holistic view of search performance and highlight areas for optimization.

**2. User Engagement Metrics:** Prerana recognizes the importance of understanding user behaviour. These insights will enable the team to personalise the search experience and improve user engagement.

**3. Revenue Metrics:** Revenue generation is a crucial aspect of the search functionality. These metrics will help the team identify revenue-driving opportunities and optimise the search process accordingly.

**4. Operational Metrics:** Ensuring the stability and performance of the search infrastructure is vital. These metrics will provide visibility into the search system's health and enable the team to address any performance issues promptly.

Building the metrics dashboard is of utmost importance due to the following reasons:

**1. Data-Driven Decision-Making:** The absence of a centralised metrics dashboard hampers the Search team's ability to make data-driven decisions. Relying on anecdotal evidence and assumptions can lead to suboptimal outcomes. Prerana recognizes that a metrics dashboard will provide real-time and comprehensive data insights, empowering the team to make informed choices.

**2. Competitive Landscape:** Wanderlust Travel operates in a highly competitive industry. To stay ahead, the Search team must continuously optimise the search experience. By leveraging a metrics dashboard, the team can identify areas for improvement, respond swiftly to market dynamics, and maintain a competitive edge.

**3. Customer Expectations:** Customers expect a seamless and personalised experience when searching for travel options. Prerana realises that by building a metrics dashboard, the team can monitor user behaviour, identify pain points, and tailor the search experience to exceed customer expectations.

**4. Resource Allocation:** With limited resources, it is crucial to allocate them effectively. Prerana understands that a metrics dashboard will enable the team to identify low-performing features or areas that require additional investment. This information will empower them to reallocate resources strategically.

Prerana is under time pressure as she has to present a prototype of the dashboard on Excel to her Product Lead in a week. Before the excitement of working on this project turns into anxiety and panic, she gets going with her work by starting to note down the key metrics that she would include in the dashboard.
