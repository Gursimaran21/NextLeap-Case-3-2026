# Case 3 : Wanderlust Travel

## Solution to Wanderlust Travel Problem Framing, Ideation & Validation + Metrics: Building a Metrics Dashboard for Wanderlust Travel

### About Wanderlust Travel

Wanderlust Travel is a well-established player in the online travel industry, catering to a global customer base. Offering a comprehensive range of travel services, including flights, hotels, and vacation packages, the company has gained significant market share. However, with mounting competition and evolving customer expectations, the Search team needs to constantly innovate and improve the search experience.

### Wanderlust Travel Dashboard Metrics Context 

Prerana Joshi is a talented Product Manager working for Wanderlust Travel, a leading online travel company. As the Product Manager on the Search team, Prerana faces the exciting challenge of building a metrics dashboard to enhance the search experience for users and drive business growth.

### Wanderlust Travel Search Team

Prerana joins the Search team at Wanderlust Travel, which is responsible for optimising the search functionality on the company's website and mobile app.

### Wanderlust Travel Search Team Problem

Currently, the team faces challenges due to a lack of a centralised metrics dashboard. Data analysis is performed manually, and insights are scattered across various systems and tools. This fragmented approach slows down decision-making and hinders the team's ability to identify and address key areas of improvement.

### Wanderlust Travel Search Team Goal

To overcome existing challenges and enhance the team's efficiency, Prerana recognizes the critical need for a comprehensive metrics dashboard. The search team goal is to create a centralized dashboard to track system health, user engagement, revenue generation, and search efficiency to keep Wanderlust Travel competitive.
